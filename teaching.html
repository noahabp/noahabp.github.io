I have twice served as a course assistant for Stanford’s MS&E 180 (Organizations: Theory and Management), earning excellent course evaluations. Beyond those duties, I have been invited to guest lecture in both MS&E 180 and MS&E 280, on strategic transitions and organizational culture.

In 2026, I gave an invited talk at Stanford’s McCoy Family Center for Ethics in Society: “Understanding and Addressing the Risks of LLMs Compromising Participant Pseudonymity in Qualitative Research.”

Earlier, at Harvard Business School, I served as a course assistant for Finance 1 and, as a research associate to Joshua Coval and Malcolm Baker, helped redesign the course’s case curriculum.
